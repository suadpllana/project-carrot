# FY2026 Supply Agreement — Term Sheet

**Buyer:** Cadence Instruments LLC, Aurora IL, USA
**Seller:** Rheinwerk Feinmechanik GmbH (supplier code SUP-2318)
**Part:** SP-40 stainless valve seat
**Contract year:** 1 April 2026 – 31 March 2027 (Buyer's FY2026)
**Status:** Offer received 2025-11-19. Signature pending award decision.

---

## 1. Price

| Item | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Unit of measure | Box of 100 pieces |
| Price | EUR 172.00 per box |
| Price basis | Firm fixed for the full contract year |
| Currency | EUR |

Seller ships in standard 100-piece boxes and invoices per box. Partial boxes
are not tendered. No escalation or surcharge applies during the contract year.

## 2. Delivery and Incoterms

**FCA Solingen (Incoterms 2020).** Seller's obligation ends on handover to
Buyer's nominated carrier at Seller's works. **Buyer arranges and pays all
inbound transportation, insurance, duties, customs clearance and brokerage**
from Solingen to Aurora IL. Buyer's freight is billed separately by Buyer's
forwarder and is not part of Seller's invoice.

Buyer issues monthly releases. Seller tenders one consolidated shipment per
calendar month, twelve (12) shipments per contract year.

Quoted lead time: 45 calendar days from release.

## 3. Payment terms

Net 30 days from date of invoice. No cash discount is offered.

## 4. Volume rebate

No minimum annual volume commitment applies.

Seller grants a banded annual volume rebate on pieces purchased in the
contract year:

| Contract-year volume band (pieces) | Rebate rate |
| --- | --- |
| 0 – 200,000 | 0.0% |
| 200,001 – 400,000 | 1.5% |
| Above 400,000 | 3.0% |

**Clause 4.2.** The rebate is calculated band by band: each rate applies only
to the portion of the contract-year volume that falls inside that band. Volume
already invoiced in a lower band is not re-rated when a higher band is reached.
The rebate is settled as a single year-end credit note in EUR and does not
alter the due date of any invoice issued during the contract year.

## 5. Quality

Seller warrants that lots tendered under this agreement will not exceed a
**maximum acceptable nonconforming rate of 2.0% of pieces at Buyer's incoming
inspection.** This figure is a contractual specification limit governing
Buyer's right to reject a lot and to invoke the corrective-action provisions of
clause 7; it is not a forecast, guarantee or estimate of the nonconforming rate
Seller will achieve in any period.

**Disposition of nonconforming pieces (clause 5.4).** Pieces found
nonconforming at Buyer's incoming inspection are dispositioned by Buyer under
its own receiving procedure. Where Buyer returns the pieces to Seller against a
return authorisation, Seller bears the return freight and replaces the pieces
at its own cost within the contract year; replacement pieces are not separately
invoiced and are not counted as pieces purchased under this agreement. Where
Buyer scraps the pieces, Seller issues no credit, no replacement and no
warranty allowance, and the pieces are a loss to Buyer. Buyer is responsible
for ordering sufficient quantity to meet its net good-unit requirement.

## 6. Term and termination

Twelve months from 1 April 2026. Either party may terminate for convenience on
90 days' written notice.

## 7. Corrective action

Two consecutive lots exceeding the clause 5 specification limit trigger a
formal 8D corrective action at Seller's cost.
