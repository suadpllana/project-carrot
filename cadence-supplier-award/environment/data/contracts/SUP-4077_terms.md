No escalation, surcharge or index adjustment applies during the contract year.

## 2. Delivery and Incoterms

**FCA Sheffield (Incoterms 2020).** Seller's obligation ends on handover to
Buyer's nominated carrier at Seller's works. **Buyer arranges and pays all
inbound transportation, insurance, duties, customs clearance and brokerage**
from Sheffield to Aurora IL. Buyer's freight is billed separately by Buyer's
forwarder and is not part of Seller's invoice.

Buyer issues monthly releases. Seller tenders one consolidated shipment per
calendar month, twelve (12) shipments per contract year.

Quoted lead time: 38 calendar days from release.

## 3. Payment terms

**2% 10, Net 60.** Buyer may deduct a 2.0% cash discount from the invoiced
value if payment is made within 10 days of invoice date. Otherwise the full
invoiced value is due 60 days from invoice date.

## 4. Volume commitment and rebate

**4.1 Minimum annual volume commitment.** Buyer commits to purchase a minimum
of **520,000 pieces** in the contract year. If Buyer purchases fewer than
520,000 pieces in the contract year, Buyer shall pay a **volume shortfall
charge of GBP 0.35 per piece** applied to the difference between 520,000
pieces and the number of pieces actually purchased. The shortfall charge is
invoiced at the end of the contract year.

**4.2 Volume rebate.** Seller grants a rebate of **4.0% of the total
contract-year purchase value**, applied to every piece purchased in the
contract year. The rebate is **earned only if Buyer purchases 520,000 pieces
or more in the contract year.** No rebate of any amount is earned if
contract-year volume is below 520,000 pieces. Where earned, the rebate is
settled as a single year-end credit note in GBP and does not alter the due date
of any invoice issued during the contract year.

## 5. Quality

Seller warrants that lots tendered under this agreement will not exceed a
**maximum acceptable nonconforming rate of 3.5% of pieces at Buyer's incoming
inspection.** This figure is a contractual specification limit governing
Buyer's right to reject a lot and to invoke the corrective-action provisions of
clause 7; it is not a forecast, guarantee or estimate of the nonconforming rate
Seller will achieve in any period.

**Disposition of nonconforming pieces (clause 5.4).** Pieces rejected at
Buyer's incoming inspection are scrapped by Buyer. Seller provides no credit,
no replacement and no warranty allowance for rejected pieces. Buyer is
responsible for ordering sufficient quantity to meet its net good-unit
requirement. Rejected pieces count toward the volume commitment in clause 4.1
and toward the rebate threshold in clause 4.2, both of which are measured on
pieces purchased.

## 6. Term and termination

Twelve months. Either party may terminate for convenience on 90 days' written
notice.

## 7. Corrective action

Two consecutive lots exceeding the clause 5 specification limit trigger a
formal 8D corrective action at Seller's cost.
