## 2. Delivery and Incoterms

**DDP Cadence Instruments, Aurora IL (Incoterms 2020).** Seller's price is
inclusive of all inbound transportation, insurance, duties, customs clearance
and brokerage to Buyer's dock. Buyer is not separately invoiced for freight on

calendar month, twelve (12) shipments per contract year.

## 3. Payment terms

Net 45 days from date of invoice. No cash discount is offered.

## 4. Volume

No minimum annual volume commitment. No volume rebate, growth allowance or
year-end credit applies to this agreement.

## 5. Quality

Seller warrants that lots tendered under this agreement will not exceed a
**maximum acceptable nonconforming rate of 3.0% of pieces at Buyer's incoming
inspection.** This figure is a contractual specification limit governing
Buyer's right to reject a lot and to invoke the corrective-action provisions of
clause 7; it is not a forecast, guarantee or estimate of the nonconforming rate
Seller will achieve in any period.

**Disposition of nonconforming pieces (clause 5.4).** Pieces rejected at
Buyer's incoming inspection are scrapped by Buyer. Seller provides no credit,
no replacement and no warranty allowance for rejected pieces. Buyer is
responsible for ordering sufficient quantity to meet its net good-unit
requirement.

## 6. Term and termination

Twelve months. Either party may terminate for convenience on 90 days' written
notice.

## 7. Corrective action

Two consecutive lots exceeding the clause 5 specification limit trigger a
formal 8D corrective action at Seller's cost.
